Shebotech Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Shebotech ("we") handles information when you contact us on WhatsApp or use our apps (StoreHub, CubsHub, Yummy).

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the messages you send us.
  • Details you choose to share, such as your name, business name, the app you are interested in, and a preferred call time.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and recommend the right app.
  • To book and hold a call with our team.
  • To follow up about your request.

Our WhatsApp assistant uses an automated AI system to reply. Your messages are processed by our AI provider only to generate replies.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers needed to run the service (Meta/WhatsApp and our AI provider), or when required by law.

Retention

We keep conversation and contact details only as long as needed to serve you, then delete them.

Your rights
